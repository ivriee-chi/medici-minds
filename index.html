MEDSCI Minds: Who we are.

MEDSCI Minds is a student-led club for those passionate about the human body works or aspiring to work in the medical field. It is more than just a club, it is a community where science and medicine meet, an educational network that gives students opportunities to explore health-related issues in their community and advocatre for meaningful change in healthcare.

MEDSCI Minds is committed to promoting student-led research. This section highlights the current projects, educational presentations, and future publications.

🧠 Opioid Addiction Research Project

In Progress

This is our first student-led research initiative which examines the neurological, social, and public health impacts of opioid addiction. The project goal is to educate students while exploring evidence-based prevention strategies and community awareness.

Status: Research in progress.
